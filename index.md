# Privacy Policy — Kiki Health AI

**Last Updated: October 12, 2026**

## 1. Overview

Kiki Health AI is a personal health data management application designed to help its owner collect, organize, and analyze health information obtained through the WHOOP API.

The application is intended for private, personal use.

## 2. Information We Collect

With authorization, Kiki Health AI may access:

- Recovery data, including recovery scores, heart rate variability (HRV), and resting heart rate.
- Sleep data, including sleep duration and sleep performance.
- Physiological cycle data, including strain and heart rate information.
- Workout data, including exercise activities and associated metrics.

## 3. How We Use Information

Collected information is used to create personal health summaries, analyze trends, and generate daily Markdown reports.

The application does not use health information for advertising or sell personal data.

## 4. Data Storage and Security

The application is designed to store health information in a private, access-controlled environment. OAuth credentials and access tokens are intended to be protected from public access.

Cloud infrastructure providers may process or store information as necessary to operate the application.

## 5. Data Sharing

The application is intended for use by its owner. Personal health information is not intentionally shared with unrelated third parties.

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion

The application owner may delete stored health records and revoke WHOOP authorization. Data retention depends on the configured storage environment.

## 7. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when application functionality or data handling practices change.

## 8. Contact

Privacy inquiries may be directed to the application owner through the contact email registered in the WHOOP Developer Dashboard.
